01Tilt to drink

Tip it. Drink it.

The whole screen is the glass. Tip it like a real pint and the beer sloshes, foams and drains as you drink. Then pass it to a friend and watch their face.

  • Tap to refill when it’s empty
  • Double-tap for a fresh pour any time
  • Shake to whip up the head
  • Pop the big bubbles in the foam
  • Youtilt left
  • Friendtilt right

02iPhone Duo

One beer becomes two.

Open an iPhone Duo and the beer you were holding becomes two, one on each half. One’s yours, one’s your friend’s. Tilt left to drink the left one, right to drink the right. Fold it shut and they pour back into one.

  • Closed: one beer on the cover screen
  • Open: two beers, one each
  • iPhone: one big beer, edge to edge
  • iPad: one big beer

03Refill a Friend

Run dry? Let friends top you up.

Share your empty glass and it pops up on a public feed. Anyone, anywhere can refill it and earn a point of Karma for being a pal. A fresh beer pours on your phone with a little “Beer from Priya” on it, so you know who to thank.

  • KarmaOne point for every glass you top up.
  • LeaderboardsKarma and Beers Finished, on Game Center.
  • Your callKeep your glass off the feed in Settings.
